How it works

  1. Mark the fields in your layout

    Draw a box where a field should go and type a tag inside it, like [Form_Text_FirstName] or [Form_Check_Terms]. Gray text in the box becomes the placeholder.

  2. Drop the PDF into the app

    The fields appear in their place, named and styled. Adjust them, add calculations or buttons, and fill in the form in test mode.

  3. Export the fillable PDF

    A preflight check points out overlaps and small fonts. The exported file is a standard PDF form that people fill in with Acrobat Reader.

No tags in your PDF? The app finds empty boxes, lines and checkboxes on its own and suggests fields for them.

What your forms can do

Checked inputs
Email, date, phone, number and postal code fields check what people type, in the formats of Europe, Switzerland, the UK or the US.
Calculations
Sums, products and formulas like Quantity * Price * 1.19 update while the form is filled in.
Fields that appear when needed
Show a field only when a box is checked or an option is chosen, for example a message field for gift wrap.
Dropdowns, checkboxes, radio buttons
Plus comb fields with one box per character for postal codes or bank numbers.
Buttons
Send the filled form by email, print it, save it as a PDF or clear it.
Your typography
One font, size and color for all fields, taken from your layout, with your own fonts if you like.
Test before you send
Fill in the form inside the app with the real tab order, then export.
Pages and projects
Merge PDFs, rotate and reorder pages, and pick up where you left off. Forms are saved in your browser and as project files.

Your files stay on your computer

PDF Form Maker runs entirely in your browser. Your PDFs are never uploaded, because there is no server to upload them to. That makes it a good fit for contracts, applications and anything with personal data.

Works with the tools you already use

Any app that exports a PDF: Affinity Publisher, InDesign, Illustrator, Canva, Word, Pages and LibreOffice. If your app exports layers, you can also put the tag in a layer name instead.

Questions

Which PDF readers can fill in the forms?

Every common PDF reader shows the fields and lets people type. Checked inputs, calculations, fields that appear when needed and the buttons use Acrobat JavaScript, so they work in Adobe Acrobat and the free Acrobat Reader on desktop and mobile. Browsers and macOS Preview show the fields without these extras.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and drop in a PDF. Your recent forms are kept in your browser on this device.

Can I use a scanned form?

Yes, but scans contain no text or shapes to detect, so you draw the fields yourself. That takes a few minutes for a one-page form.

Can I change a form I already exported?

Yes. Open the exported PDF again and its text fields, checkboxes and radio buttons are kept, ready to be moved, renamed or extended. To keep every setting, such as calculations and buttons, also save a project file from the app.

Will the form look like my design?

The fields sit exactly on your boxes and take over your font, size and color. Tags and gray hint text are hidden in the export, so the printed layout stays as you designed it.
